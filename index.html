Every tee time in the Twin Cities.
One search.

TeeSheet TC pulls live tee times from more than 140 public golf courses across the Minneapolis–St. Paul metro and western Wisconsin into one screen. Pick your date, time window, and how far you'll drive. See every open time, sorted by price or distance. Tap to book directly with the course.

Free to use. No account required. We never take a booking fee.

How it works

Twin Cities courses are spread across a dozen-plus different booking sites. Finding a Saturday-morning time means opening them one at a time. TeeSheet TC does that for you.

Pick when and where

Choose a date, a time window, and a radius from your location (or any spot on the map).

See everything at once

Every open tee time from every course in range, in one list. Sort by price, time, or distance. Filter by 9 or 18 holes and party size.

Book with the course

Tap a time and you land on the course's own booking page. Your reservation is with them, at their price. We're never in the middle.

Spot the deals

Discounted times are flagged right in the results, so the cheap Tuesday twilight round doesn't hide.

Favorites and map view

Save the courses you play most. Browse everything on a map when you'd rather explore than search.

Public Country Club filter

PCC member? Flip one switch and see only participating courses, so every result is one you can use your membership at.
