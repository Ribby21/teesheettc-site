Support

Questions, feedback, a course we're missing, or something that looks wrong? Email teesheet@ribby.dev. It's a one-person operation, so you'll get a reply from the person who built the app, usually within a day or two.

Common questions

A tee time in the app wasn't available when I tapped through.

Tee times refresh roughly every 30 minutes, and popular times go fast — someone else may have booked it between our last check and your tap. The course's booking page is always the source of truth. If a course is consistently wrong, tell us and we'll look at that course's feed.

Why isn't my course in the app?

We cover every public course we can reach across the Twin Cities metro and western Wisconsin. A handful of courses don't offer online booking at all, so they're listed with a phone number instead of live times. If a public course is missing entirely, let us know — adding courses is the most common request and usually quick. See the full list.

How do I cancel or manage Pro?

Subscriptions are handled by your app store, not by us. Android: Google Play → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → TeeSheet TC. iOS: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → TeeSheet TC. Refunds follow Google's and Apple's policies.

I subscribed but still see ads or the search limit.

Open the app's Settings and tap Restore purchases. If that doesn't fix it, email us with the platform (Android or iOS) and we'll sort it out.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts. Favorites and preferences live on your device. See how to delete your data and our privacy policy.

Are you affiliated with the courses or GolfNow?

No. TeeSheet TC is independent. We read publicly available tee-time listings and send you to the course's own booking page. We never take a booking fee and never handle your payment.
